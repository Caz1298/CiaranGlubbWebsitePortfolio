Colour Of Mystery:

Description:


Colour of Mystery was a project that I worked on as a group in Unity for one of my third-year classes. The game saw the player move through a building while avoiding guards to find the hidden documents in the main office. The player was equipped with a paint gun that, depending on the colour, would have different effects, such as being able to see through walls or power different objects. My main role in this project was to work on the level design and the AI guards detection/Movement. For AI detection, I used a plugin called RayCast Pro, and for movement, I used Unity’s NavMesh and a self-made guard AI state machine. The other members of my group consisted of two other programmers and two artists. Overall, the project could have been made a bit better, as there were a few problems during its development and my level design could have been better, but it still created some great learning opportunities and experiences.

Skills Gained:


  • Basics of Level Design and Planning
  • Basic Modelling in Maya
  • AI State Machines and Movement
  • Unity’s NavMesh
  • AI Sight Detection
