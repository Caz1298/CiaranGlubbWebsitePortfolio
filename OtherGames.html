Ciaran Glubb / Game Development Portfolio

Factory Horror:

Factory Horror was a project that I worked on in Unity for one of my third-year classes. It saw the player move through an abandoned factory looking for files while avoiding the human-looking monster in the building. This project was mainly targeted at teaching us about enemy detection and tracking, as well as having us create our own AI state machines. We also had to learn about Unity animation controls and systems, with us placing in different animations we found on the internet.

Read Threads:

Red Threads was a game that I worked on in Unity over the course of a 48-hour game Jam with the theme entanglement. This game saw the player try to find connections between random photos/images on a conspiracy board, where every level gets bigger and more confusing. My role was creating what each of the random connections were and creating a paper friend that helps the player by providing clues and info when clicked. The other members of my group consisted of two other programmers and two artists.

Robot Escape Room:

Robot Escape Room was a project that I worked on in Unity for one of my second-year Classes. In this game, the player has to solve puzzles and find hidden robotic parts that were missing from their body and put them back on themselves. The project was for a VR course, meaning it was made through the use of Unity’s VR controls and free plugins. This project was mainly targeted at teaching us about making games for VR systems and the different ways in which you have to look at making a game in the field compared to traditional controls.

Teleporting Game Idea:

This Teleporting Game was just a random game concept that I had and did a bit of work on in Unreal Engine. I wanted the player to be able to pick up different objects that were a range of different sizes or weights and then throw them. Then, while in motion, the player can choose to teleport (i.e., swap with the object) and keep moving forward in the direction the object was going. This is a small project that I had an interest in, and I worked on it in my free time.

Magic Hat:

Magic Hat was a game created in Unity as part of a one-week-long Game Jam during my fourth year of university. In this game, the player investigates a murder case inside an old mansion full of puzzles and secrets. The player is also equipped with a magic hat that they can teleport to after it’s thrown. My role was creating the different puzzles featured in the game, as well as the character's movement and object grabbing/moving. The other members of my group consisted of two other programmers and two artists.

2D Dungeon Game:

2D Dungeon Game was a project created in C++ code as part of one of my third-year classes. In this game, the player had to move between different rooms in a dungeon, solving puzzles, collecting items, attacking enemies and finding keys to get to the end. This project was targeted at teaching us proper coding practices and layouts within C++, but also to take into other coding languages, before moving on to Unreal Engine for our next assignment.
